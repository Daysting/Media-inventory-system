# DaystingInventory Support

DaystingInventory helps you organize books, video games, movies, electronics, borrowers, and loans on your Mac. The app may appear on your Mac as **Daysting’s Home Inventory System**. Version 1.6 requires macOS 13 or later and supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

## Getting started

1. Select Books, Video Games, Movies, or Electronics in the sidebar and add an item.
2. Add a borrower before recording a loan.
3. Use **Checkout/Return** to record loans and returns.
4. Use **Dashboard** and **Reports** to review your inventory and lending activity.

No separate app account is required.

## Back up or move your inventory

Open the app’s **Settings** and choose **Export Inventory…** to save a portable backup. Use **Import Inventory…** to restore a saved inventory. Importing replaces the active inventory after making a recovery copy; review the confirmation before continuing.

Backups include borrower details and embedded cover images. Keep them private and retain a backup before importing, troubleshooting sync, or changing Apple accounts. Use **Open Sync Backups** in Settings to find local recovery copies.

## iCloud sync

Optional sync requires iCloud Drive and the same Apple Account on each Mac. Sync is enabled by default when available; you can turn it off in the app’s Settings. Select **Sync Now** and wait for **Up to date** before switching Macs.

If sync is unavailable, your local inventory remains usable. Check your internet connection, iCloud Drive settings, available iCloud storage, and the sync status shown in the app.

If two Macs make conflicting edits, the app asks which inventory to keep. It preserves copies for recovery, but it does not merge individual records. Review the choices carefully: selecting one inventory replaces the other, including changes unique to it. Export a backup before resolving a conflict if you are unsure.

Turning off sync does not delete existing copies from iCloud or other Macs.

## Images and notifications

Use **Choose Image…** to select a cover stored on your Mac. If an older image link no longer works, select the image again. Supported cover images must be smaller than 8 MB.

If notifications do not appear, check the app’s notification permission in macOS System Settings.

## Report a problem

Please include:

- Your macOS version and the app version/build number.
- What you were trying to do and the steps that reproduce the problem.
- The exact error message, if one appears.
- Whether iCloud sync was enabled, if relevant.
- A screenshot with personal information removed, if it helps explain the issue.

Do not post inventory databases, borrower details, private images, passwords, or Apple Account credentials in public GitHub issues. Do not send a full database unless needed for troubleshooting and you have removed information you do not wish to share.

For information about local storage, iCloud, and support communications, read the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
