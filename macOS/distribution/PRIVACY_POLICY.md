# Privacy policy draft

This draft describes the code prepared for version 1.6. The publisher must confirm it, add a support contact and effective date, and publish it at a stable public URL before submission.

Daysting Home Inventory stores inventory entries, borrower details, lending history and cover images locally on your Mac. The app does not include advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs and does not send your inventory to the developer.

If you enable iCloud inventory sync, your inventory database, including borrower details and cover images, is stored in your Apple iCloud account and synchronized to Macs using that account. iCloud is operated by Apple and is subject to Apple’s terms and privacy practices. You can disable sync in Settings; doing so does not delete copies already stored in iCloud.

If you supply an internet address for a cover image, the app contacts the host at that address to retrieve it. That host can receive the normal network information associated with the request. You can instead choose an image stored on your Mac.

Inventory exports and conflict backups include the database’s contents. You decide where exported files are saved and who receives them. Local conflict backups are retained until you remove them. Removing an item from the active inventory does not erase previous backups or exports.

The app can request permission to display local notifications. Development builds may optionally index inventory and borrower information in Spotlight; this is disabled by default. No app-specific account is required.

For questions or help managing your data, contact the publisher using the support details provided on the app’s App Store page.
