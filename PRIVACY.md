# DaystingInventory Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 25, 2026**

This policy explains how DaystingInventory for macOS (also named Daysting’s Home Inventory System) handles information. The app is published by Erick Hofer / Daysting.

## Information stored by the app

DaystingInventory stores the information you enter, including inventory descriptions, purchase details, cover images, borrower names and contact details, and lending history. This information is stored in a database on your Mac and is used to provide the app’s inventory and lending features.

The app does not require a separate Daysting account. It does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs, and it does not send your inventory database to Daysting. Daysting does not have access to your inventory simply because you use the app.

## Optional iCloud sync

When iCloud sync is enabled, your inventory database—including borrower details, lending history, and embedded cover images—is stored in your Apple iCloud account and synchronized with Macs using that account. iCloud is operated by Apple and is subject to [Apple’s privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

iCloud sync is enabled by default when available. You can turn it off in the app’s Settings. Turning it off does not delete copies already stored in iCloud or on other Macs. Daysting does not receive a copy of your inventory through this sync feature.

## Cover images and external services

You can choose a cover image from your Mac. Images embedded in the inventory are included in database backups and iCloud sync.

If you enter or import an internet address for a cover image, the app may contact that address to retrieve the image. The host can receive your IP address and normal request information, and its own privacy practices apply. Choose a local image if you do not want the app to contact an external image host.

Apple services, including the App Store, iCloud, and any system diagnostics you choose to share with Apple, are governed by Apple’s settings and privacy practices.

## Backups, retention, and deletion

Your active inventory remains on your Mac until you remove its data. You can edit or delete records in the app and export a backup from Settings. If sync is enabled, inventory changes can also be synchronized to iCloud.

Exports, import recovery copies, and sync conflict backups can contain the full inventory, including borrower details and images. Deleting an item from the active inventory does not erase older backups, exports, iCloud copies, or copies on other Macs. Local recovery backups remain until you remove them; use **Settings → Open Sync Backups** to find them. You control where you save exports and with whom you share them.

To remove all copies, also review your saved exports, local recovery backups, other Macs, and iCloud storage. Contact support if you need help identifying the app’s files. Daysting cannot directly delete inventory stored only on your devices or in your Apple account.

## macOS features

The app may request permission to show local notifications. You can change notification permissions in macOS System Settings.

Optional local Spotlight indexing, when enabled, lets macOS index inventory and borrower information on your Mac. It is off by default.

## When you contact support

If you email or call Daysting, you may share your email address, phone number, message, and any information you choose to provide. We use that information to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue. Email and telephone service providers process those communications as part of delivering their services.

GitHub issues are public and are subject to [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Do not post inventory databases, borrower details, private images, passwords, or other personal information in a public issue. You can contact us about privacy questions or request deletion of support correspondence.
